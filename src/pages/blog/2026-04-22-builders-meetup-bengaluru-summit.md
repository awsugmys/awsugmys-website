Heading to AWS Summit Bengaluru? Let's grab lunch! ☁️🍴

To all fellow builders from the AWS UG Mysuru community — since many of us are heading to KTPO for the Summit, let's take a moment to sync up in person!

This isn't a formal event — just a casual "say hi" session for anyone from our community who happens to be there.

---

📍 **Where:** The Main Lunch Area (depends on the crowd!)

🕒 **When:** April 22–23, 2026 | KTPO Exhibition Center, Whitefield

🍕 **What:** A casual meet & greet and a chance to grab lunch together!

---

⚠️ **Please Read (The Important Stuff)**

- **Entry:** This is held inside the Summit. You must be a registered attendee with an official AWS confirmation to enter.
- **Disclaimer:** This is not an official AWS UG Mysuru organized event. It is a voluntary, informal gathering.
- **Responsibility:** The User Group and its leaders are not responsible for registration, entry, food, or travel. It's strictly "come if you can, stay if you like!"
